## A

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original TokenMini logo concept sheet, square 1536x1536.
Create an unconventional, highly disciplined premium software brand mark for TokenMini, a quiet Mac utility that aggregates AI usage and machine status. Theme: continuous sensing, counting, aggregation. The mark must feel new through its structure, not decorative sci-fi effects.
Concept A: ONE FOLDED LOOP. An almost circular continuous band with an ingenious rounded return/fold creates an implied capital T in the central negative space. Do not place a typeset T inside a circle. The outer silhouette is gently asymmetric yet balanced. It should read as one precisely constructed object with a surprising junction; no sharp inward cusp or protruding tip. Keep the void open and legible. Avoid a conventional donut, power button, infinity symbol, or three-blade swirl.
Art direction: flat, crisp, vector-friendly shapes; no texture, no 3D, no gradients, no shadows. Warm ivory background and generous space. Upper two thirds: one large pure black monochrome mark, fully visible. Lower third: a small duplicate of exactly the same mark with one structural segment colored fresh emerald green, followed by the word "TokenMini" in refined quiet sans-serif. Small corner caption "A / FOLDED LOOP". No other text or mockups. The black and green versions must have identical geometry. Design the mark, not a poster of unrelated shapes. Research inspired the method of deriving a letter from a structure; do not imitate an existing brand logo.

## B

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original TokenMini logo concept sheet, square 1536x1536.
Design an original unconventional minimal logo for TokenMini, a Mac utility unifying AI usage and computer state. It needs a future-oriented intellectual feel from a new structural relationship, clear enough to later simplify for a menu-bar icon.
Concept B: SEGMENTED WHOLE. Exactly three substantial rounded geometric units fit into a near-circular compact whole, as if tiny token units have aggregated into one instrument. Their asymmetrical interlocking relationship and one continuous negative-space channel suggest a capital T on second glance. No literal T superimposed; no standard T cut out of a disc. Avoid three equal pie slices, loading spinners, dashboard gauge, coins, crypto marks, pebble/cell logos, or triangular teeth. Give the components custom matching curved end faces and a deliberate surprising fit. Crisp silhouette with a few large readable voids. No thin details.
Layout: warm ivory square concept sheet, spacious. Upper two thirds one large black monochrome mark, all three units black. Lower third: small exact duplicate with only one unit emerald green, the other units black, next to the exact text "TokenMini". Small caption "B / TOKEN UNITS". Flat vector-like solid color only, no metal, no 3D, no gradients, no shadows, no decorative backgrounds. Geometry must match across both versions. Professional graphic identity exploration, not a corporate icon template. Do not copy any reference logo.

## C

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original TokenMini logo concept sheet, square 1536x1536.
Create an original experimental but practical logo for TokenMini, a quiet precise Mac menu-bar tool aggregating AI usage and system information.
Concept C: PAIRED SECTIONS. Two bold smooth arc-like sections with different inner and outer curvatures form a compact almost circular symbol, leaving a continuous T-shaped negative space at their junction. Think of an unexpected cross-section of an engineered object, and two information streams meeting. The T should be discoverable without being drawn as a standard font glyph; explore positive/negative figure-ground ambiguity. Two coherent physical parts only, gentle rounded terminals, no projecting points. Asymmetry of the joining boundaries creates character, while the whole stays optically balanced. Clearly distinct from a generic ring enclosing a T. No eye, shield, power icon, navigation pointer, infinity loop, generic spiral, or coin.
Art direction: pure black flat graphic on warm ivory, refined highly original logo exploration. No gradients, shadows, 3D or texture. Spacious square sheet 1536x1536. Upper two thirds one large monochrome symbol. Lower third one small identical symbol with one of its two structural segments emerald green and the other black, plus the exact word "TokenMini". Small corner caption "C / PAIRED SECTIONS". The two symbol versions must have identical geometry. No other writing, no scene, no app screenshots.
