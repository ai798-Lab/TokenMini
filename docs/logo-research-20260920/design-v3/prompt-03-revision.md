# 03 重做：错位栅格

初稿退回原因：落入常见柱状信号图形，与此次突破常规模板的要求不符。

Use case: logo-brand. Create a remarkably distinctive experimental flat black logo for "TokenMini", an intelligent macOS instrument that samples AI usage and computer state. This direction is a DISPLACED SAMPLING GRID. The main symbol is compact and nearly square but has no outer container: imagine an abstract solid sampled into four carefully shaped asymmetric fragments on a two-by-two grid, with one fragment shifted just enough to create an unexpected continuous negative-space passage. Each fragment should have a precisely drawn cut-out or stepped edge; the four pieces collectively form one striking silhouette. Combine very straight edges with a small number of generously curved interior cuts. Make the balance taut, editorial, intelligent and unusual, like experimental contemporary glyph design. Not a letter monogram. The elegance must come from proportions and the conversation between solid and void. It must be legible as a strong black shape when small. No skinny details. Avoid radial or rotational symmetry.

Output a restrained horizontal brand design sheet with an entirely opaque pale ivory paper background filling the whole canvas. One large master symbol, a carefully spaced exact "TokenMini" wordmark with quiet narrow typography, and below them a small duplicate of the identical symbol in green. Show only this one selected logo, not a grid of alternate concepts. Flat black vector-like shapes, perfectly sharp edges. Large amount of whitespace. Absolutely no bars ordered by height, equalizer, wifi, circles, flowers, asterisks, rings, arrows, building skyline, 3D, gradients, glowing dots or mockup icons. Do not make a retro stripe mark. No text except the name "TokenMini". This proposal must stand on its own in black on ivory and look radically different from a signal-strength icon or a folded spatial ribbon.
